Key Features

QR Code — Book & View Appointment

Patients can scan a QR code from any invoice or prescription to instantly book a new appointment or view their upcoming scheduled appointment date — no login required.

Manage Patient Details

Patients can enter and manage their personal health records. Dedicated tabs display complete information including medicaments, diseases, lab tests, and medical history.

Generate Reports

Generate comprehensive PDF reports for patient details, appointment history, and prescriptions — giving clinics and patients a clear, printable summary at any time.

Generate Patient Prescriptions

Create detailed prescriptions containing both doctor's and patient's information. Directly generate an invoice from any prescription with a single click.

Automated Appointment Reminders

Patients automatically receive a reminder email one day before their scheduled appointment, reducing no-shows and keeping both patients and doctors informed.

Clean & Simple Interface

The appointment module provides both Calendar View and List View for efficient scheduling and management. The interactive weekly calendar displays time-based appointment slots, allowing staff to easily track and manage patient bookings. A built-in mini calendar and search functionality provide quick navigation and filtering, ensuring a smooth and user-friendly experience.

Configuration title

Follow the steps below to configure the Physiotherapy Management System after installation.
  1. Install the module from the Odoo Apps menu.
  2. Navigate to the Physiotherapy Management menu in the main dashboard.
  3. Configure clinic staff and healthcare professionals from the Staffs section.
  4. Create patient records with required medical and contact information.
  5. Schedule appointments using the calendar or list view.
  6. Generate prescriptions and invoices directly from patient appointments.
  7. Use the reporting features to track patient history and clinic activities.
Once configured, the system allows clinics to efficiently manage appointments, patient records, prescriptions, and billing in one place.
